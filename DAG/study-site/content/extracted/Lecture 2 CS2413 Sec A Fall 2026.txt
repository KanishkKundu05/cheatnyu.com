                Bubble Sort Analysis revisited
Alternatively, could show worst-case run-time is O(n 2) with a less exact
calculation:

BUBBLESORT(A, n)
for i = 1 to n − 1           \\ do this at most n times
   for j = n downto i + 1                \\ do this at most n times
        if A[ j] < A[ j − 1]
             exchange A[ j] with A[ j − 1]




                                         41
                Bubble Sort Analysis revisited
Then, could show worst-case run-time is Ω(n 2) with a less exact calculation:

BUBBLESORT(A, n)
for i = 1 to n − 1           \\ for each i that is between 1 and n/2
   for j = n downto i+1               \\ do this at least n/2 times
        if A[ j] < A[ j − 1]
             exchange A[ j] with A[ j − 1]

Since runtime is both O(n 2) and Ω(n 2), it is θ(n 2)

❖   Theta (sometimes called Big-Theta)
         Intuitively, f(n) = θ(g(n)) means they are asymptotically equal
❖   By definition, f(n) = θ(g(n)) means
     ❖     f(n) = O(g(n))
     ❖     and f(n) = Ω(g(n))
❖   Rule of thumb for getting Theta notation for expression that is sum of terms
     ❖     Remove lower order terms and multiplicative constants
❖   e.g.
     ❖     f1(n) = 38n 3 + 7n 2 + 1
     ❖     f2(n) = 10500n 3 + 4n 2 + 24
